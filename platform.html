How Customer Connect Desk works

A straightforward flow connecting platform administration, each business and its customers.

1. A business is registered and approved

Platform administration configures the store, supported features, public content and storefront presentation.

2. The business uses its digital touchpoints

Customers can open the public shop page or reach the configured check-in experience through QR and NFC links.

3. Customer activity is organised

Supported check-ins and visit workflows are recorded for the relevant business and made available through authorised dashboards.
