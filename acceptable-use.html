Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Acceptable Use Policy describes the basic rules for accessing and using Customer Connect Desk (CCD), including its public business pages, digital customer check-in features, QR/NFC touchpoints and authorised business dashboards. It applies to platform users, registered businesses, shop operators and visitors.

1. Lawful and responsible use

You must use CCD only for lawful purposes and in a way that does not interfere with the platform, other businesses, customers or the public. You are responsible for ensuring that the information, offers, products, services and communications you submit or publish are accurate and lawful.

2. Prohibited activities

  • Using CCD to facilitate fraud, scams, impersonation, harassment, threats or unlawful discrimination.
  • Uploading malware, malicious code, phishing links or files intended to compromise users or systems.
  • Attempting to bypass authentication, access another business's records, enumerate customer accounts or interfere with tenant isolation.
  • Scraping, harvesting or misusing customer names, contact details, visit records or other personal information.
  • Sending unsolicited bulk messages, spam or communications without the required permission or legal basis.
  • Publishing counterfeit, stolen, unsafe, illegal or misleading products, services, reviews or promotions.
  • Abusing QR/NFC links, APIs, upload endpoints, rate limits or automated systems.
  • Misrepresenting a business's identity, location, authorisation, pricing, availability or customer reviews.

3. Business and content responsibilities

Businesses are responsible for the content they provide, including names, descriptions, images, videos, contact information, prices, opening status, public policies and promotional material. Businesses must have the necessary rights and permissions to use uploaded content and must not publish another person's personal information without an appropriate basis.

4. Customer information and communications

Users must provide accurate information when submitting a check-in or enquiry. Businesses must use customer information only for legitimate purposes connected with the relevant service or relationship, respect recorded communication preferences, and comply with applicable privacy, consumer-protection and messaging requirements. A WhatsApp draft or email submission status must not be represented as proof that a message was delivered or read unless the relevant delivery confirmation is actually available.

5. Security and account access

Keep sign-in credentials, PINs, verification codes and access links confidential. Do not share administrator or merchant credentials. Notify the platform operator through its official support channel if you believe an account, device or business workspace has been compromised. Do not probe, scan or test systems without prior authorisation.

6. Monitoring, restrictions and suspension

CCD may investigate suspected misuse and may restrict content, disable a feature, suspend a business or limit access where reasonably necessary to protect users, data, the platform or legal compliance. Where appropriate, the operator may request information to review a report or suspected violation. This policy does not guarantee uninterrupted availability or a particular search-engine or social-platform listing.

7. Third-party services

CCD may use or link to services operated by third parties, including hosting, mapping, messaging, email, analytics and search services. Their own terms and privacy practices apply when you use those services. CCD cannot guarantee the availability or operation of third-party services.

8. Reporting misuse

To report suspected abuse, privacy concerns, security issues or content that appears to violate this policy, use the current official contact or support channel published by Customer Connect Desk. Include the relevant shop URL, a description of the issue and supporting details. Do not include passwords, OTPs or unnecessary sensitive personal information in a report.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the platform, applicable requirements or security practices. The date above identifies the latest published revision. Continued use after an updated policy is published indicates that you have had an opportunity to review the revised terms.

10. Important operator details

Customer Connect Desk is the platform name used on this website. Before production publication, the operator should complete the website's separate Terms of Service and Privacy Policy with its verified legal entity name, registered/business address, grievance or privacy contact, data-retention details and applicable governing-law provisions. No unverified company registration, postal address or email address is stated on this page.

This policy is a platform-use draft and is not a substitute for legal advice or a complete Privacy Policy / Terms of Service. Have the final legal documents reviewed for the actual operating entity and jurisdictions before production launch.
