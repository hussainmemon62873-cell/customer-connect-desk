Data Retention & Deletion Policy

2. QR retirement and serial reuse

When a QR code is retired, its old scan token must remain invalid. If the visible serial is later selected for reuse, the new allocation receives a new scan token. Historical records should retain only the fields needed to establish the prior issue and retirement, prevent accidental reuse and investigate security or ownership disputes.

3. Deletion and anonymisation requests

Send a request to [PRIVACY / GRIEVANCE EMAIL] with the registered email and allocation reference. Do not send an OTP, owner PIN or password. We may ask for additional information to verify the request. We will erase or anonymise eligible personal data when the purpose is complete, subject to applicable retention duties, unresolved disputes, security investigations and other lawful grounds.

4. Statutory logs

Where applicable, the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 require certain personal data and associated logs to be retained for at least one year for specified purposes, unless another law requires otherwise. The Operator must map this requirement to the actual logs and data processed by this service and must not treat it as permission to retain every record indefinitely.

5. Backups and third-party systems

Deletion from the live spreadsheet may not immediately remove data from backups, provider logs or retained audit records. The Operator must document backup rotation, access restrictions and the process for allowing deleted data to expire from backup systems.
