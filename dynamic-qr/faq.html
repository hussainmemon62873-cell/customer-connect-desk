Dynamic QR Platform: Frequently Asked Questions

What is a dynamic QR code?

A dynamic QR code points to a managed scan address. An authorized operator can change the destination behind that address without regenerating the physical QR pattern, subject to the scan service remaining active.

How do I access my QR allocation?

Open the customer portal, enter the allocation reference and the registered email address, then enter the one-time verification code sent to that email.

Can I see another customer's QR codes?

No. The customer session is associated with one registration and its QR allocation. Do not share your email verification code or portal session.

Can I change my URLs after approval?

Customers submit their initial destination mapping for owner review. After approval, destination changes are controlled by the owner. Contact the platform support address for help.

Does the owner approve each QR separately?

The intended workflow is one approval for the complete initial customer allocation. The owner reviews all submitted QR destinations before approving the set.

What if my QR is suspended?

A suspended QR should show an unavailable message instead of redirecting. Contact support if you believe the suspension is an error.

Can a deleted QR serial be reused?

A visible serial may return to the reusable inventory. The reissued serial receives a fresh scan token, while the old scan token remains retired.

What file formats can I download?

The customer portal supports individual PNG downloads, a ZIP of QR images and text records, and a CSV inventory list, subject to browser and library availability.

Does a dynamic QR work offline?

No. Scanning and opening an online destination requires internet access and an available redirect service.

Where can I get support?

Use the Contact us link shown in the portal when a support email is configured. The operator should publish a monitored support address before launching the service.
