TRUST & SAFETY

Acceptable Use Policy

PRE-LAUNCH DRAFT — Add the monitored abuse-report contact and response process before publication.

Every QR destination must be lawful, accurately described and safe for the people who scan it.

1. Permitted use

Use the service for legitimate business, product, service, event, education, menu, support and information destinations that you own or are authorised to publish.

2. Prohibited use

  • Phishing, credential theft, malware, scams or fraudulent payment requests.
  • Impersonating a person, business, government body or other organisation.
  • Deceptive redirects, cloaking, malicious downloads or harmful scripts.
  • Unlawful tracking, harassment, doxxing, privacy violations or unlawful content.
  • Copyright, trademark or other intellectual-property infringement.
  • Attempts to bypass access controls, interfere with service operation or access another customer's data.

3. Destination checks

HTTPS and private-network checks reduce some technical risks but do not certify a destination as safe. QR owners must inspect the destination, keep it accurate and re-check it after changes. Scanners should verify the destination shown by their device before entering credentials or making payments.

4. Reporting abuse

Report a suspicious QR code or destination to [MONITORED ABUSE EMAIL]. Include the QR serial, scan URL if available, destination URL, time and a concise explanation. Do not send passwords or verification codes.

5. Enforcement

The Operator may investigate reports, suspend or retire a QR destination, restrict an account, preserve relevant records and notify appropriate parties where reasonably necessary for safety, legal compliance or incident response. Restrictions may be applied while a report is investigated.
