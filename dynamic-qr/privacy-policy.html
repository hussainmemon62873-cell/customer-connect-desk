This Policy explains how the Operator handles personal data when you register for, access or use the Dynamic QR Platform. We aim to collect only information needed to allocate QR codes, verify access, operate the service, maintain security and provide support.

2. Personal data we may process

  • Registration details: name, mobile number, email address and age (collected to enforce the platform’s 18+ eligibility rule).
  • Account and allocation details: customer ID, registration reference, QR serials, allocation status and customer verification status.
  • Destination details: URLs submitted for QR codes, approval status, destination changes and suspension or deletion records.
  • Security and operational records: authentication events, audit history, verification-code request metadata, scan time and limited scan analytics such as referrer or device category where available.
  • Support communications: information you provide when asking for help or reporting abuse or a security issue.

Do not submit sensitive personal information in a destination URL or support message unless it is necessary and you have a lawful basis to do so.

3. Purposes of processing

We use personal data to create and manage customer records; allocate QR inventory; send and verify one-time email codes; show a customer only their own QR collection; receive and review destinations; enable approved redirects; protect accounts and QR tokens; prevent abuse; maintain audit and ownership history; troubleshoot errors; respond to requests; and comply with legal obligations.

4. Consent, notices and your choices

Where consent is the applicable basis, we will present a clear notice describing the data collected and the specific purposes before or at collection. You may withdraw consent through [DESCRIBE THE ACTUAL WITHDRAWAL METHOD]. Withdrawal does not affect processing already lawfully carried out, and some information may need to be retained for security, dispute or legal reasons. Do not collect optional fields unless their purpose is explained.

5. Service providers and sharing

The current architecture is designed to use Cloudflare Pages and Pages Functions for website hosting and API proxying; Google Apps Script and Google Sheets for backend processing and storage; Google Identity Services for owner sign-in; Google MailApp for verification and service emails; and third-party content-delivery networks for browser libraries used by QR and export features. Confirm the exact providers, enabled features, processing locations and applicable vendor terms before publication. We do not authorise providers to use personal data for unrelated purposes where they act as our processors.

We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users or the service, to investigate suspected abuse, or as part of a lawful business transfer with appropriate safeguards.

6. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, subject to applicable legal, security and dispute requirements. The Operator must publish the actual schedule for customer profiles, registrations, QR ownership history, scan/security logs, support records and backups in the Data Retention Policy. Relevant security logs may be subject to statutory minimum retention requirements.

7. Security safeguards

The service is designed with owner authentication, an additional owner PIN, email verification for customers, server-side authorisation, destination validation, opaque scan tokens, audit records and restricted access to the database. These measures are intended to reduce risk; they do not guarantee that every incident or unauthorised access attempt can be prevented. See our Security & Incident Response page.

8. Personal data breach response

If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will assess and respond to it, take reasonable steps to contain and mitigate harm, preserve relevant evidence, and notify affected individuals and the appropriate authority in the manner and within the timelines required by applicable law. A notice will describe the nature of the incident, likely consequences, mitigation steps, recommended precautions and a contact for assistance, as required.

9. Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to information about processing, correction or erasure of personal data, withdraw consent, raise a grievance or nominate another person where legally permitted. Send requests to [PRIVACY / GRIEVANCE EMAIL]. We may need to verify your identity before acting. The Operator must add a practical request method and response process before launch.

10. Children and age eligibility

The Dynamic QR Platform is intended only for individuals who have completed 18 years of age. We do not knowingly accept registrations for, or intentionally collect personal data from, individuals under 18. The person creating a customer allocation must confirm that the customer has stated they are at least 18. If we learn that an account relates to a person under 18, we will restrict the account and take appropriate steps to delete or otherwise handle the information in accordance with applicable law. Contact the privacy address above if you believe an under-18 person’s information has been submitted. This age restriction is a product eligibility rule; it does not replace any legal obligations that may apply to the Operator.

11. Cookies, local storage and third-party scripts

The application uses browser functionality and may load Google Identity Services and third-party libraries for QR generation and file exports. Advertising and analytics must remain disabled unless the relevant vendor disclosures and consent controls are implemented. See the Cookie Policy.
