Cookie & Browser Storage Policy

PRE-LAUNCH DRAFT — Verify actual browser storage and third-party requests in the live deployment. Add consent controls before enabling non-essential analytics or advertising.

1. Essential browser functionality

The application uses browser memory and standard browser storage/network functionality to support sign-in, customer verification, QR rendering and file exports. The current frontend keeps owner and customer session tokens in runtime JavaScript memory rather than deliberately persisting them in local storage. Confirm this behaviour against the deployed build before publication.

2. Third-party scripts

Depending on the page, the site may load Google Identity Services for owner sign-in and browser libraries from public CDNs for QR generation, ZIP creation and spreadsheet exports. These providers may receive technical request information such as IP address and browser metadata when their scripts are requested. Add the current vendor privacy links and any changes in provider usage here.

3. Analytics and advertising

Advertising and analytics are disabled by default in this release. Do not enable them until the publisher identifiers, vendor disclosures, cookie inventory and any required consent mechanism are configured. Ads must not be shown on owner sign-in, customer verification, customer dashboard, owner dashboard or security/error screens.

4. Your controls

You can use browser settings to block or clear cookies and site data, but doing so may sign you out or affect service functionality. Where consent is required, provide an accessible method to change or withdraw that choice.
