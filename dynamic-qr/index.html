DYNAMIC QR CODE MANAGEMENT

What is a dynamic QR code?

A dynamic QR code is a printed, scannable code that points to a managed scan address. The destination behind that address can be updated by an authorized operator, so the same physical QR artwork can continue to be used when an approved destination changes. This is different from a traditional static QR code that stores its destination directly in the QR pattern.

This platform separates the visible QR serial from its scan token. A QR code is assigned to a customer allocation, receives a destination submission, and stays inactive for redirect purposes until the owner approves the complete allocation. The owner can later manage approved destinations without asking the customer to reprint a new code for every link update.

Dynamic QR codes for business workflows

Use managed codes for product information, service instructions, menus, event details, campaign pages, feedback links and other destinations that may need maintenance over time. Always test the final printed code before distributing it.

Static QR vs dynamic QR

A static QR stores the destination in the code itself. A dynamic QR points to a managed redirect address. Dynamic codes can be more flexible, but depend on the redirect service remaining active and configured.

Destination approval and control

Customers submit their initial URLs for review. The owner checks the entire QR collection, then approves it once. Protected owner controls manage later edits, suspensions and retirements.

QR inventory and reissue history

Visible QR serials can be moved into a reusable pool. A reissued serial receives a new scan token, while the previous scan token stays retired to avoid old printed links silently becoming a different customer's code.

PRACTICAL QR CODE ADVICE

How to use dynamic QR codes reliably

Before printing: use a short, trusted HTTPS destination, maintain strong contrast, keep a quiet zone around the QR pattern, avoid stretching the image, and test the actual print at its final size. Keep a readable serial label beside the code so support teams can identify it.

After printing: keep the owner account secure, review destination changes, check that the target page works on mobile, and retire codes that are no longer authorized. Do not place QR codes where a scan would mislead users about the destination or the business responsible for it.

Frequently asked questions

Does every QR need separate owner approval?

No. The owner approves the customer's entire initial QR allocation once, after every QR has a destination URL.

Can customers change URLs after approval?

No. After initial approval, destination fields are locked. The owner can unlock and change destinations after PIN verification.

What happens to deleted QR IDs?

They move to the Deleted / Reusable inventory. When selected for a new allocation, the ID is removed from that list and transferred to Active inventory.

Will an old printed QR redirect to a new customer after its serial is reused?

No. The visible serial and secret scan token are separate. A reissued serial gets a fresh scan token; the old token remains retired.
